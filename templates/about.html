About StudyAI

StudyAI is an AP practice and study-planning app.

Quizzes

Select a subject and topics, then create a multiple-choice quiz. Save an unfinished quiz, submit it for a score and explanations, or retry missed questions.

Question banks cover selected topics in six subjects. These are original practice questions, not full AP exams or predictions of an exam score.

Courses and study plans

The catalog includes topic guides for {{ catalog|length }} AP courses. Study plans use your written-practice results and topic confidence ratings to suggest what to review. Quiz results are saved in a separate history.

AI tutor and written practice

These optional features require an AI connection. Ask for help with a question or generate a written problem and submit your solution for feedback. Check AI answers against your notes or course materials.
