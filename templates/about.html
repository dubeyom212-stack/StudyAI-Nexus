About StudyAI 🌌

An AI-powered learning intelligence platform that helps students understand how they learn.

Our Mission

Students often know they need to study more, but they don't know what concepts they are missing or why they struggle.

StudyAI uses diagnostics, AI analysis, and personalized recommendations to turn studying into a data-driven process.

How It Works

1. Diagnose

Analyze strengths, weaknesses, and knowledge gaps.

2. Understand

Find the root causes behind mistakes.

3. Improve

Create a personalized learning strategy.

Technology

Built with Python, Flask, databases, and AI learning systems.
