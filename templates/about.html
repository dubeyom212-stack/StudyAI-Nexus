ABOUT STUDYAI

Practice that helps
you figure it out.

Sometimes you just need a few questions to see what you know before a test. That is where StudyAI starts.

Pick a subject and try a quiz.

Choose your topics and the number of questions. When you finish, you can see your score, read the explanations, and try the questions you missed again. You can save an unfinished quiz and come back later.

Quizzes currently cover six subjects. The course catalog has 26 AP courses, but we are still building out the question banks. These are practice questions, not official AP exams or predictions of your exam score.

Want to write out your thinking?

The optional tutor and written-practice pages use AI. When a tutor is connected, you can ask for help or get feedback on your reasoning. AI can get things wrong, so check anything that seems off against your class notes.

Quiz results stay in your quiz history. Written-practice results feed into the study plan and mistake notebook.
