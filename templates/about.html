WHY THIS EXISTS

Advice should give you
something to do.

“Study more” isn't much of a plan. Work through a question, understand what went wrong, and try a better next step.

A practical study loop

Choose a course and a topic. Try an original practice question. Get feedback against three criteria, then use your mistake notebook to revisit the specific concept you missed.

Your study plan puts missed concepts first, schedules a review after three days, and introduces untested topics. Optional confidence ratings help you get started.

AI connection

The tutor supports local Ollama models without API charges, or an optional paid OpenAI connection. Local models require a separate installation on the app's server. If the connection fails, the app tells you and keeps your submitted work.

Know what the results mean

Practice questions and feedback are AI-generated. They can contain errors and are not official AP questions, grades, or score predictions. Topic guides are introductory study maps, not a complete course curriculum.
